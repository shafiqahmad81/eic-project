What is PCI DSS Compliance?

Our PCI DSS compliance approach is holistic, addressing every aspect of payment data security—from card data discovery and network segmentation to vulnerability management and access control. As organizations face evolving threats and regulatory demands, we provide tailored compliance roadmaps, ensuring efficiency and relevance at every stage. Our experts also perform ASV scans, VAPT, and continuous monitoring to maintain adherence and safeguard customer trust.

Ensure Safe Payments. Maintain Standards. Build Trust.

PCI DSS standards ensure companies that handle credit card data—acceptance, processing, storage, or transmission—maintain a secure ecosystem. EIC offers comprehensive solutions to achieve and maintain PCI DSS compliance, safeguarding your business and customer trust.

Our PCI DSS Compliance Services Include

Comprehensive solutions for achieving and maintaining PCI DSS compliance.

PCI DSS Audit & Certification

We conduct official assessments led by Qualified Security Assessors (QSAs) to validate your organization’s PCI DSS compliance. Our team prepares the formal Report on Compliance (ROC), the document required by card brands, ensuring your business meets all mandated security standards with confidence.

PCI DSS Readiness Assessment

Our comprehensive readiness assessment performs a detailed gap analysis of your current security posture against PCI DSS requirements. By identifying areas that need attention before the formal audit, we help you reduce audit time, minimize costs, and ensure a smoother compliance journey.

Vulnerability Management & Scanning

We conduct regular internal and external vulnerability scans to detect and remediate security weaknesses across your systems. This includes mandatory ASV scans keep your infrastructure compliant with quarterly PCI requirements while maintaining a proactive security posture.

Penetration
Testing

Through simulated real-world attacks on your networks, applications, and personnel, we uncover critical vulnerabilities that automated scanners may miss. Our penetration testing helps you address security gaps before they can be exploited, strengthening your overall payment data security.
